# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to making participation in OpenSocrates a respectful,
welcoming, and harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of
background, identity, experience, ability, or viewpoint.

## Expected behavior

Participants should:

- be respectful, patient, and constructive;
- critique ideas and code without attacking people;
- acknowledge uncertainty and correct mistakes openly;
- respect privacy and avoid sharing another person's sensitive information;
- give credit for ideas and contributions;
- accept reasonable maintainer decisions about project scope and safety.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, threats, discriminatory language,
sexualized attention, deliberate intimidation, doxxing, sustained disruption,
and publishing private communications without permission.

## Scope

This code applies in the repository, issue tracker, pull requests, discussions,
and other spaces where someone represents the project.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns to the maintainer through
[ParkerHwang's GitHub profile](https://github.com/ParkerHwang). Do not place
sensitive personal details in a public issue. Project maintainers may edit or
remove contributions and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation
when behavior violates this code.

Maintainers will consider context, impact, recurrence, and the safety of
affected participants when deciding on a response.
