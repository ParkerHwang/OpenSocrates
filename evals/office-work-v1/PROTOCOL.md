# Case pack use protocol

This protocol concerns the authored fixtures. Follow the lab's platform setup,
isolation preflight, attempt budget and final-output approval screens as well.
The owner operates the real app and makes practical judgments. The lab prepares
inputs, observes the dedicated outbox and records approved artifacts; it does not
run actors, inspect their thinking or send messages to fictional recipients.

1. Approve the case pack and its common fixed B instruction before the first
   attempt. If the situations are implausible for your work, stop expansion and
   revise a future version; do not tune active inputs after seeing answers.
2. Use the two calibration cases first to check preparation, exact requested
   model/effort, output intake and whole-answer review. Count failed attempts and
   retain them. Calibration is not evidence of improvement.
3. For discovery, use the four different cases with identical task materials and
   final boundaries in A, B and C. Use a fresh independent conversation and the
   assigned isolated environment for every attempt. New folders alone do not
   remove account/global instructions, plugins or memory. Unknown isolation is
   labeled exploratory; known contamination blocks a clean comparison.
4. Copy the displayed prompt without condition-specific edits. If the app asks
   a task question, provide only that case's fixed `clarification_reply`. Count
   the question, not its text. Do not supply extra facts or conversational coaching.
   A different follow-up interaction belongs in a separately designed study.
5. Keep the final body, audience-facing message, explicit conditions and any
   requested author memo together in `out/final.md`. The case's `deliverable`
   defines this boundary before generation. Do not keep hidden thinking or tool
   traces, and do not remove a relevant caveat, structure or identifying footer
   to make an answer look blind. Preview and approve only synthetic final content.
   Manual paste into the outbox is allowed when marked as manual intake.
6. Prefer reviewing a completed batch later. Compare whole answers before any AI
   explanation or fact checklist. Choose an alias, a tie or all unusable. A first
   reaction is optional; “I cannot explain why” is valid. Record confidence,
   recognition and whether the comparison reproduced your usual experience.
   The operator may remember conditions, so this is partial blinding, not a
   double-blind or independent human assessment.
7. Lock the first judgment before revealing conditions or evaluator facts. The
   checks identify factual commitments and uncertainty; they are not a style
   rubric, preferred answer or score that overrides personal usefulness. Keep
   any later approved edit or analysis distinct from the original answer and
   first preference. Do not replace an unfavorable first attempt with a retry.
8. Freeze human-confirmed criteria and record the discovery reference before
   exposing the two confirmation cases. If the reviewer already read or helped
   tune either case, use a newly authored pack/version instead of calling it a
   fresh holdout. The authoring process alone cannot enforce reviewer non-exposure.
9. Report each operating-system/host/model/effort cell separately. Include blocked,
   failed, incomplete and unattempted assignments plus diagnostic retries. Do not
   pool a win rate across different models or infer general-user effects from the
   owner's decisions. Similar usefulness for B and C leaves additional framework
   benefit unconfirmed; convenience needs separate evidence.

All source materials are public synthetic fixtures, not permission to collect
private real-work conversations. Never add accounts, client documents, real names,
credentials, screenshots, raw prompts from other work, transcripts or hidden
reasoning. Final answer approval is not a provider retention guarantee. Do not
paste real sensitive material into optional comments or edits. Export is a
separate explicit local action; it is not public upload or publication.

If the tested situations do not reproduce the difference the owner normally
feels, record what is missing without forcing a dimension score. Revise the next
case pack or design a separate natural multi-turn study. Do not claim a benefit
because the software completed all steps or several AI advisers agreed.
