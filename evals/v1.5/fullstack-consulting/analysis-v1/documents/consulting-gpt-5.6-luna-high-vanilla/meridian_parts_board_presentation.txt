METHOD
Scenario economics are transparent—and deliberately limited
6
Annual incremental contribution
C × volume uplift + U × (1 + uplift) × per-unit 
saving − annual fixed cost
Stress overlay
3% of gross sales refund shock; plus 10% of net sales 
depreciation shock in PLN/CZK markets; then 25% uplift..
What the model does not claim
• No causal demand estimate
• No statistical confindence or probability of scenarios
• No DCF, ramp, tax, finnancing, synergy or capacity 
model
• No proof that population/GDP drives parts demand
• ECB rates are analytical reference translations
Decision implication: use the model to rank options and set gates, then refresh with observed pilot evidence 
before full rollout.
