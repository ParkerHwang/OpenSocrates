90-day implementation and gates
Days 0–30 | CFO + Commercial Operations: join orders, credits, carrier lanes, cost and returns; review invoice
samples. Gate 1: ≥98% record and cost coverage, FX and cutoff reviewed, Finance signs baseline. Dependency:
operational-system access.
Days 31–60 | COO + Regional Service: validate catchment, addressable units, avoidable lane savings and delivery
baseline; obtain lease, labor, carrier and fit-out quotes; design reversible pilot. Gate 2: economics remain positive in low
volume and joint stress, within €450k and seven FTE.
Days 61–90 | CEO/Board sponsor + CFO: stage release after named owner, SLA, inventory controls and signed costs.
Gate 3: approve pilot/fit-out only if evidence passes; otherwise defer with dated review.
Market context: World Bank population changed Spain +2.22%, Czechia +2.18%, Poland -0.71% from 2022–24. 2024
GDP/capita was $67,465 in Netherlands and $25,104 in Poland (current US$); context only, not demand proof.
Weekly KPI: OTIF vs baseline, delivery hours, expedite EUR/order, return rate/restock recovery, fill rate, eligible units,
contribution after recurring hub cost, capex forecast and FTE. Owners: Service, Supply Chain, Commercial Ops,
Finance and HR. Risks include synthetic assumption transferability, quote inflation, stock obsolescence, delayed
recovery, FX and ramp.

Evidence, accounting and limitations
Resolve highest revisions, include shipped non-test 2025 orders including free shipments, attach returns only through
2026-01-31 and quarantine unlinked IDs. Sale month ECB mean is local units per EUR; per-order components half-up to
cents. Restocked units recover cost. Quality: 1328 order rows, 13 duplicate rows, 1254 eligible; 241 return IDs attached,
1 quarantined. All 72 month-country rows reconcile. Client data/options are synthetic. Frozen official-source data: ECB
and World Bank; WB lastupdated 2026-07-13, captured 2026-09-27. Missing values not imputed. Scenarios are
assumption-driven, non-causal and undiscounted. No interviews or live account access. See source-register.csv and
evidence/raw for source trail.
