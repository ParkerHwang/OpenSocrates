90-DAY PLAN
Use staged gates to turn assumptions into evidence
0–15 days Finance + COO Reconcile baseline, avoidable cost, pilot cohort and KPI definitions Gate: auditable baseline
16–35 days Ops + Data Costed design, service zone, SKU scope, staffing, legal and inventory 
dependencies
Gate: budget and controls
36–65 days Country + Supply Chain Reversible 3PL/temporary pilot; compare against matched baseline Gate: ≥4 weeks valid data
66–90 days CFO + COO + Board Update scenarios, stress and quotes; release, reconfigure or defer Gate: positive base 
contribution
Weekly: incremental orders/units, return rate, contribution after returns, avoidable EUR/unit, OTIF, P90 delivery, fill rate, stock accuracy, capex and FTE.

RISKS AND SWITCH CONDITIONS
Decision is conditional; reopen it on measured unit economics
What changes the choice
• Verified volume response below modeled 10–40% 
uplift
• Savings prove not avoidable or fixed costs exceed 
quotes
• Return shock reduces net contribution
• Pilot fails delivery, fill-rate or stock-accuracy gates
• Actual capex/FTE exceed board limits
Risk controls
• Matched pilot/holdout before causal lift claims
• Validate savings against actual invoices
• Separate booked revenue, cash settlement FX and 
contribution
• Limit initial SKUs; monitor restocked share and 
inventory accuracy
• Defer or reconfigure if downside is not acceptable

SOURCES AND LIMITATIONS
Appendix: model boundaries and reproducibility
Synthetic inputs: two order extracts, revisions/corrections, returns, unit costs, hub options and policy from the frozen source room.
Public inputs: archived World Bank population and GDP per capita; ECB daily reference rates, all with original URLs, timestamps and 
hashes in sources/source-register.json.
Reproduce: python3 analysis/build.py. Workbook contains 72 monthly rows, six country totals, 24 PLN/CZK monthly FX means, market 
context, all hub scenarios and all feasible alternatives.
Limitations: one synthetic baseline year; monthly ECB reference translation is not transaction FX; no causal hub results, probability 
distributions, ramp, discounting, synergy, tax, working capital or site/legal quotes. Historical public series may reflect later revisions.
