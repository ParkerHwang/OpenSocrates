SWITCH CONDITION
CZE uplift threshold: 22.2%
Holding ESP and every other input constant, if the validated CZE volume uplift is below this
threshold while POL reaches the policy's 25% base uplift, POL+ESP overtakes on modeled
annual contribution. The supplied model assumes the same uplift across countries.
What could reverse the recommendation
Lower CZE order density or savings; higher actual site/fixed cost; staffing constraints; or a
board preference for stress resilience rather than base-case return. Defer remains valid if
validation fails.
No causal effect or statistical certainty is claimed. The scenario policy specifies assumptions, not observed hub outcomes.
