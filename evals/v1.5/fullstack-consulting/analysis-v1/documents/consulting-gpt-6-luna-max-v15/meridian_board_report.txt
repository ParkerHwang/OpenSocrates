VS. NEXT BASE-RANKED: POL + ESP
CZE+ESP adds €11.8k/year in the base case, €6.1k in low and €17.5k in high; it also
adds €2.9k/year under the defined stress. It needs €15k less capex, one fewer FTE,
and its base payback is 1.14 vs 1.29 years. It is the stronger choice across the supplied
scenario values and resource use.
VS. STRESS-RESILIENT: NLD + ESP
NLD+ESP earns €107.6k/year under stress vs €32.2k for CZE+ESP, but its base gain is €31.2k
lower; it needs €45k more capex and one more FTE. Stress payback is 2.51 vs 6.99 years. A
board prioritizing the defined joint stress over base-case return could select NLD+ESP.
CONCLUSION CARD | CONDITIONAL FUNDING RECOMMENDATION
Recommendation: authorize up to €225k and five FTE for CZE+ESP, with staged release after
live operating validation. The base estimate is computed from the synthetic 2025 contribution
pool and option assumptions; it is not causal proof.
Accepted risk: stress contribution is positive but simple stress payback is about seven years.
Missing inputs: real site quotes, local operating costs, validated order density/uplift, observed
savings, and a board risk preference. Reopen if these move the ranking or annual contribution
below the low case.
Observable flip condition: holding ESP and all other inputs fixed, if CZE's actual uplift is below
22.2% while POL achieves 25%, POL+ESP overtakes on base annual contribution. Equivalent
CZE savings threshold: about €1.41/unit at both sites' 25% uplift.
Defer condition: if day-90 validation cannot support the option's low-case economics, release no
second-stage capex and return to the board. Scenarios are not probabilities and the joint stress
is not a forecast.
