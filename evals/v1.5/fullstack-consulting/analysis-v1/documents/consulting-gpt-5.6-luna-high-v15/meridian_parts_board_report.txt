European service-hub expansion | board decision report
Decision basis: 2025 shipped sales and returns known through 31 January 2026; frozen source room collected 27 September 2026 UTC. All client exports and hub
assumptions are synthetic. World Bank and ECB series are archived public snapshots.
Recommendation
Fund a staged two-hub pilot for CZE + ESP, with year-zero capex of EUR 225,000 and 5 FTE, subject to the 90-day gates below. The
base policy case implies EUR 198,099 annual incremental contribution and 1.14-year simple payback; this is a planning estimate, not a
proven causal effect.
The choice is supported by trade-off analysis: the selected pair is the highest base-case feasible alternative. It gives up the lower capex
of single-hub options in exchange for more modeled contribution. The strongest alternative is POL+ESP at EUR 186,329 annual
incremental contribution. Defer remains valid if the pilot cannot validate the volume uplift/savings assumptions or the stress case is
judged more credible.

Accounting and evidence reconciliation
The order extracts contain 1,328 rows. Exact repeated rows were collapsed, then the highest numeric revision per order_id was
retained; 43 post-revision rows were excluded for status/test/date rules. Free or zero-price shipments were retained (12). Returns were
deduplicated by highest revision per return_id, added across legitimate IDs, and limited to eligible linked orders received by the
inclusive cutoff. 1 orphan return(s) were quarantined and 1 late return(s) excluded; neither was joined by guess.
For EUR orders the rate is 1. For PLN and CZK, each sale and its summed refund use the original sale month’s arithmetic mean of
available ECB business-day reference observations, quoted as local currency units per EUR. Individual monetary components are
half-up rounded to cents before summation. Gross sales are booked sales after discount; cash can differ because refunds/settlement
timing and FX differ; contribution further subtracts net COGS and nonrefundable fulfillment.

workbook.

Hard constraints are EUR450,000 year-zero capex, seven FTE, and at most two hubs. Annual incremental contribution excludes
capex; payback is simple undiscounted capex divided by positive annual contribution. Pairs add country arithmetic with no synergy.
The stress column is the policy’s joint stress, not a probability forecast.
What changes the decision
The recommendation should be reopened if measured pilot evidence cannot support the assumed uplift/savings run-rate; if actual
capex or staffing breaches the hard budget; if return behavior or PLN/CZK FX makes the defined stress case the more credible
operating case; or if a stronger alternative becomes demonstrably executable at lower risk. Under the defined stress, NLD+ESP is the
strongest alternative at EUR 107,630 annual incremental contribution, versus EUR 198,099 for the selected base leader. Those are
observable gates, not claims about statistical certainty.

Risks and mitigations
Demand uplift risk: validate incrementality during the pilot and hold capex until Gate 3. Return-rate risk: use return rate and returned
units as explicit stop metrics; do not assume every return recovers product cost. FX risk: preserve sale-month ECB translation and
monitor PLN/CZK variance; the stress case is a guardrail. Execution risk: stage staffing and site commitments; use the hard FTE/capex
limits. Evidence risk: synthetic client inputs and no interviews mean this is a decision support model, not field validation.
Limitations and reproducibility
No customer interviews, live account access, demand elasticity study, competitor data, labor quotes, tax/legal review, actual
transaction FX, or discounted cash flow were available. The raw source room is saved under evidence/raw with SHA-256 hashes and
original public URLs. Run python3 scripts/build_deliverables.py to regenerate metrics.json, the XLSX, this PDF and the PPTX from
saved inputs.
