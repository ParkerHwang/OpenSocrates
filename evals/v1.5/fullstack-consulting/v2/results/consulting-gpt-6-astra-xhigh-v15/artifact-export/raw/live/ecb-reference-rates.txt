L35:   *   *   *   *   * # Euro foreign exchange reference rates
L36: 
L37: The reference rates are usually updated at around 16:00 CET every working day, except on cite28†TARGET closing days .
L38: They are based on the daily concertation procedure between central banks across Europe, which normally takes place around 14:10 CET. The reference rates are published for information purposes only. Using the rates for transaction purposes is strongly discouraged.
