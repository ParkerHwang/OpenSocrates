# Scope of the final provisional synthesis

This is a descriptive integration procedure, prepared while opaque evidence
assessments are still running and before analytic unblinding. It is not a new
judge rubric, a held-out protocol or a repair of historical outcomes. The fixed
judge procedure, original first-pass locks and versioned execution manifests
govern the assessments.

The analysis entry point must verify all 60 first passes, all 60 evidence-phase
assessments, their exact input identities, preserved first-pass objects, attempt
receipts, cleanup and final global lock before opening the treatment maps. It must
include the two retained A01 assessments and all 58 continuation assessments once.
Failed/interrupted calls stay in the execution ledger even without a rating.

Report every cohort, lane, task, condition and language in the joined JSON. Keep
guide-2 outcomes, guide-3 diagnostics, guide-4 diagnostics, H02 delivery probes,
Luna–Sol contrasts, same-model Sol/Astra comparisons and wrapper ablations distinct.
The coding domain consists of coding-transition, coding-invoice and developer
collaboration task families. All other families are general work. No lane summary
may pool coding and general ratings. Bilingual collaboration is also split by
developer/nondeveloper family.

Report all six anchored dimensions separately. Histograms, rated/null/NA counts
and medians are the primary ordinal descriptions. Available-value means are
descriptive conveniences only; they do not make the scale interval-valued.
Do not create a composite quality score, significance test, causal effect or
noninferiority estimate. Do not choose convenient packets or drop failures.

At the packet level, any failed critical gate means `fail`; otherwise any
unassessable gate (or no gates) means `unassessable`; only the remaining packets
have all recorded gates passing. This conservative AI summary is not human
acceptance. Preserve the actual gate lists because judges may differ in their
granularity. Never average away a critical failure. Compare original deterministic
flags with these provisional flags without rewriting either.

For EN/KO differences, join only the same cohort, lane, task family, treatment,
solver model and repetition. Require exactly one packet per language. Show each
language score and Korean-minus-English difference when both are observed; keep
null differences when either is missing. These are paired descriptions of authored
pilot cases, not estimates of language-wide effects.

Inspect cited critical findings and disagreements against packet evidence. Assess
clarification across every retained public message. The 52 main packets contain
designated artifacts and final public messages, not full dialogue; two diagnostic
packets explicitly lack messages because of a historical parser failure. A
missing earlier question is unassessable when those earlier messages are absent.
Keep any integrator correction or disagreement separate from the locked model
scores. Separate behavior defects, rubric defects and missing review evidence.

The resource ledger includes every review/transport attempt across v1–v3, with
nulls preserved. Cached input is part of input; reasoning output is part of output.
Summed call wall time is not elapsed wall time because calls overlap. Review
resources must not be added to candidate-efficiency comparisons. No billed cost
or independent backend identity is fabricated.

Reassess the factual support for each intended claim. One provisional assessor
configuration over selected pilot tasks does not establish independent human
reliability, a representative task-quality distribution or a practical loss
tolerance. The method may identify defects and measurement gaps without selecting
a numerical margin, promoting profiles or approving release. Human scores remain
unavailable, and their absence does not prevent completion of this AI-review lane.
