# Held-out study readiness after the bounded pilot

The EVAL-01–EVAL-05 engineering pilots have outcome receipts, but none is a
held-out study or a basis for validating the candidate profiles. The frozen
[protocol](protocol.json) deliberately leaves held-out numerical margins and
sample size open until feasibility and pilot variance can support an intended
claim. Do not edit its pre-outcome fields to make the completed pilots appear
preregistered as held-out results.

## What the pilot settled

- The ChatGPT desktop-bundled `codex-cli 0.155.0-alpha.16.3` completed exact
  Luna, Sol, and Astra requests at medium effort. The older system CLI 0.145.0
  access failures were client-specific. Per-run token categories are exposed;
  billed cost and independent backend model echo are not.
- A pinned installed candidate package can deliver an explicit memory pack in
  a disposable profile. The revised guide repaired one concrete recall-envelope
  failure in a new paired run. Pack application and native hook delivery have
  no universal native receipt.
- The current fixtures are easy enough that EVAL-02 coding/planning and
  EVAL-03 mechanical arms all passed deterministic gates. EVAL-04's nine cells
  also passed. EVAL-05 nondeveloper strict artifact checks exposed failures.
  One replicate per selected cell cannot establish a quality gap, an efficiency
  distribution, or noninferiority.

## Missing inputs to a defensible freeze

1. **Outcome distribution:** add independently authored tasks across the stated
   general, coding, fresh-session, and bilingual strata. Repeat enough pilot
   cells to estimate failure and resource variance without editing the
   completed records. Keep the guide-revision-1 and revision-2 treatments
   separate.
2. **Memory isolation:** show the chosen Codex client/profile condition has no
   transferable native project memory across fresh tasks, or preregister the
   comparison as confounded and restrict the claim. A new local `CODEX_HOME`
   and `--ephemeral` remove prior local state but do not independently prove
   absence of account-side context.
3. **Independent quality review:** retain privacy-safe blinded artifact packets
   and obtain repository-competent coding review plus bilingual human review
   for natural collaboration. Model-only scores may supplement but must remain
   labelled provisional. The earlier proxy/native EVAL-05 runs discarded raw
   synthetic artifacts, so they cannot be retrospectively human scored.
4. **Resource accounting:** continue to capture all attempts, repairs, retrieval,
   scans, failed tool calls, wall time, and separate input/cache/output/reasoning
   categories. Establish actual billing visibility before a monetary savings
   claim; subscription-limit movement is not an invoice.

Once those inputs exist, freeze a **new immutable held-out protocol** with the
exact task manifest/hash, model/effort/client/package identities, permissions,
strata, sample size, numerical quality and noninferiority margins, primary wall
measure, critical-failure rule, independent judge procedure, exclusion and
missing-data handling, and stop/repair rule. Only then run held-out cells. A
source or package test, a short response, or a single passing pilot may not
substitute for that outcome evidence.
