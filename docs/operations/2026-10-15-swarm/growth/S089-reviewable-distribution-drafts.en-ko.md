## FU-EN — First-use guide

### Before you forward an AI-written proposal

I maintain OpenSocrates. This guide was prepared with AI assistance.

An AI-written proposal can sound ready to send while its central assumption
still needs checking. To explore that problem, start with the proposal task in
the [English/Korean first-use worksheet](https://gist.github.com/ParkerHwang/d814da73fb445f7123eb5d637e912cd5).
It uses a made-up work situation, so you can try it without sharing company material.

1. Follow the [v1.5.2 setup guide for your app](https://github.com/ParkerHwang/OpenSocrates/blob/v1.5.2/docs/setup-guide.md).
   Ordinary Claude Chat and Cowork use the account skill ZIP; Claude Code uses
   a separate local integration. Preserve existing instructions and back up an
   older skill before replacing it. If you already have a working setup, start
   with the task. Keep a deliberately disabled setup disabled unless you choose
   to enable it.
2. In a fresh conversation, use worksheet task 2, about a proposal that has no
   demand evidence yet. You can add this working-note request:

   > Before I circulate this, give me a brief note with: what the supplied facts
   > support; one assumption that matters most; and one observation that could
   > challenge it. Keep proposed research separate from research already done.

3. Read the answer before forwarding anything. Can you see which statements are
   supported and which remain assumptions? Is the suggested evidence step
   something you could actually carry out? Choose any next action yourself;
   a suggestion does not approve research or launch a product.

This is a task to try. No answer from this task was generated for this guide,
and it does not demonstrate improved results. Claude account-skill selection
can be missed. Installation, activation, actual use and a useful answer remain
separate observations.

If you choose to share feedback, a non-sensitive summary of your app, product
version, unclear step and whether the answer was useful is enough. Do not post
private documents or your full conversation.

1. [쓰는 앱에 맞는 v1.5.2 설정 안내](https://github.com/ParkerHwang/OpenSocrates/blob/v1.5.2/docs/setup-guide.ko.md)를
   따릅니다. 일반 클로드 채팅과 Cowork는 계정 스킬 ZIP을 쓰고, Claude Code는

   의도적으로 꺼둔 설정은 직접 켜기로 선택하기 전까지 유지하세요.

   > 이 제안을 공유하기 전에 짧은 검토 메모를 써줘. 주어진 사실이 뒷받침하는
   > 내용, 가장 중요한 가정 하나, 그 가정에 의문을 제기할 수 있는 관찰 하나를

   직접 선택하세요. 제안이 나왔다고 조사나 제품 출시가 승인된 것은 아닙니다.

직접 시도할 과제 안내입니다. 이 안내를 위해 해당 과제의 답변을 생성하지
