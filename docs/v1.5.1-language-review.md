# v1.5.1 Chinese and Japanese content review

The v1.5.1 localization adds Simplified Chinese (`zh`) and Japanese (`ja`) to
the existing 48-method foundation. English and Korean authored procedures and
cases remain unchanged. The four-language content identity is revision 4.

## Review scope and method

Six independent AI reviewers each compared eight methods across English,
Korean, Chinese and Japanese, including complete procedures, examples, method
metadata, three teacher questions per locale and catalog entries. Two additional
reviewers compared the shared Chinese and Japanese instructions, presentation
policies, guides, host templates and installation documentation. Review checked
eligibility, contraindications, public outputs, numerical limits, evidence states,
stop conditions, handoffs, meaningful grammar and the distinction between
optional and required actions. The nine English procedure headings and machine
identifiers are protocol fields and intentionally remain in English.

Writers and reviewers read the baseline OpenSocrates controller, decision guide
and complete eligible canonical procedures before applying them. Reviewers used
`critical-thinking` at content revision 3 from the released English/Korean
foundation. The parent also used the native source decision path. Native
application remains `unverified`; reading, review and model-reported attribution
do not establish an authenticated application receipt or answer-quality gain.
This document records public findings and corrections, not private reasoning.

## Findings and corrections

| Finding | Correction |
| --- | --- |
| Chinese hold language could mean retaining a test or release recommendation | Explicitly pause unauthorized tests and withhold release recommendations; use clear deferred-judgment wording without changing genuine keep/retain instructions |
| Required output pairs in catalog summaries became alternatives | Preserve both controls and owner, metric and threshold, decision and chance nodes, source and status, cost and delay, and related required pairs |
| Japanese design-thinking prohibition reversed replacement direction | State that design thinking must not replace an established technical diagnosis |
| Chinese authority and unresolved objection wording changed meaning | Use decision authority rather than prestige, and an unresolved objection rather than an already-valid objection |
| Japanese robust-decision-making used a transaction relationship for trade-off | Restore the explicit trade-off |
| Chinese live test and time-pattern wording narrowed scope | Refer to real-world testing and patterns over time without inventing real-time or long-term requirements |
| Japanese reader guidance blurred unknown versus absence | State explicitly that an omitted fact is unknown and omission is not evidence of nonexistence |
| Japanese attribution instructions demanded reading all methods | Require reading the applied eligible procedure in full |
| Example terminology or grammar changed the task | Correct diagnostic statistical odds, service access versus authorization, deterministic execution and native question grammar |
| Japanese steelman wording lost English optionality | Retain the optional Conclusion Card presentation in the new translation; preserve historical English/Korean sources |

Initial review examined implementation `49b0157c8f29a7bf151ed9fa8cb0342d4c8d4dc3`.
The listed corrections have been integrated; targeted reviewer rechecks and final
runtime/package gates are pending. The PR records the final reviewed commit,
exact verification commands, results, publication and remaining evidence limits.

## Boundaries

Chinese support means Simplified Chinese. Language detection is a script
heuristic: Han-only input defaults to Chinese and may be Japanese; an explicit
Japanese choice resolves that ambiguity. Account skill export remains separate
from upload, activation and model selection. Platform and live-host qualification
remain those of v1.5.0. Windows ARM64 and the Go migration belong to v1.6.0.
This review adds no broad rewrite of the original English/Korean method bodies,
new hidden-reasoning logs, or universal model-quality claim.
