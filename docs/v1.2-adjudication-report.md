## 4. Blind packet contents

Each packet contains: pair ID, EN and KO case text, legacy case kind, legacy
owner method, legacy expected route and assertion, authoring intent for both
locales, authored decisive features, the public definition sections (Purpose,
Use when, Do not use when, Stop conditions) of the owner method and every
candidate method named in the queue item plus the two clarification
candidates, the selector ordering contract, the adjudication questions, the
candidate resolutions, and an empty decision form.

Each packet excludes: selector outputs, selected method lists, model
rationales or instructions, per-case pass/fail, effort levels, aggregate
recall, arm comparisons, and any projection of score impact. The builder
enforces this structurally — it aborts on any of 20 forbidden key names
anywhere in the packet body, and separately asserts that the decision form
starts empty.

## 8. Role separation status

The preparation work in this report was done in the **packet preparer** role,
which the protocol permits for a party that is not eligible to adjudicate.
Concretely, this session read case texts, legacy labels, the queue, the
protocol and migration documents, and hashed the raw result files without
reading their contents; it made no gold decision and pre-answered none of the
ten policy questions or four boundary questions.

The remaining phases require people this session cannot supply:

- **Primary adjudicator** — a human who has not seen #65 case-level outputs or
  aggregates. Must lock the §5 policy answers first, then all 51 pairs.
- **Second reviewer** — a different human, also unexposed, submitting an
  independent decision before comparison.
- **Resolution reviewer** — needed only for substantive disagreements.
- **Label author / intent witness** — may explain authoring intent, may not
  adjudicate.

An AI agent cannot fill the independent adjudicator role here, and no
automated tool in this change promotes a candidate resolution to a gold label.
