## 3. Review agreement and policy resolution

The two complete reviews agreed compatibly on only two boundary pairs and had
49 substantive disagreements. This is itself an important diagnostic: the
original insufficiency concept did not imply one obvious selector policy.

The main disagreement was systematic:

- Primary treated nearly every insufficiency pair as optional intervention and
  attached all five behavior classes to most pairs.
- Second distinguished explicit method contraindications from method stop
  conditions and classified each method contract separately.

For provisional development use, the maintainer-authorized synthesis adopts
this product rule:

1. A matching **Do-not-use** condition is a contraindication: the method must
   not be selected.
2. A matching **Stop condition** with one identifiable owner permits and,
   under the route-identification selector contract, requires
   `route_owner_then_hold`.
3. A clarifier leads only when clarification is itself the repair route; it is
   otherwise an inclusion method behind the owner.
4. Multi-leading and optional-intervention cases are not leading-metric
   eligible unless one unique leader is fixed by policy.
5. Undetermined, unresolved, rewrite, invalid, or material translation-mismatch
   pairs are excluded from policy metrics.

Primary dissent is preserved for all 49 substantive disagreements in
`adjudication-disagreements-v1.0.0.jsonl`; no reviewer result was overwritten.

Three explicit resolution adjustments were made:

- The mechanical flowchart pair uses the independently recovered Claude record:
  no-method execution and bounded deduction are both valid.
- `reflective-equilibrium-insufficiency-01` and
  `triangulation-insufficiency-01` were normalized from prohibited intervention
  plus `bounded_analysis` to prohibited intervention plus
  `hold_no_intervention`, because the former combination violates the selector
  contract. Their supported direct limitations remain in their rationales.
